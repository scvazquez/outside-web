Outside privacy notice (testing)

Last updated 2026-11-17. This notice covers the testing phase.

What Outside is

Outside helps friends find time to go out together. It is invite-only during the first release.

Your choices

  • Delete your account. Type the word DELETE in the app. Your access stops at once. After a 7-day waiting period we remove everything that identifies you: your profile, sign-in identities, friends, blocks, Circles, windows, plans you joined, calendar data, location, push tokens, feedback and AI tool grants. Your messages in other people's groups stay as messages with no author, so the conversation still reads correctly for the others. A deletion request can be undone only during the 7 days, by contacting support.
  • Get your data. You can ask for a JSON file of your own data. It holds what you wrote and own. It never holds another person's private data. There is no screen for it in the testing phase.
  • Block, remove, revoke. Blocking a person, removing a member and revoking an AI tool take effect at once and survive a restore from backup.
  • Location. You choose when to start and stop sharing for each plan.
